# Member Booking Participant visibility

My Bookings grants read access to a Booking's Booker and the current primary or secondary members of its Active Ministry, evaluated at read time. Participants may read complete booking information, including the Booker's name and email, but not payment instructions or audit identities; lifecycle history does not disclose a replacement Ministry or activity. Only the Booker may perform member self-service actions, so Ministry participation does not confer cancellation, payment-instruction, or rebooking authority.
